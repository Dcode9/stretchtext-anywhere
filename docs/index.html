Deepen

In 1967 Ted Nelson described stretchtext: text that grows where the reader wants more and shrinks back where they don't. Writing every level by hand was too much work, so it never caught on. Generated text makes it cheap.

Deepen is a browser extension that brings this to every web page. Click a sentence to grow it; click again to shrink it. Expansions come from a Pollinations text model and are paid with your own Pollen.

Try the idea

Stretchtext lets a single page hold many reading depths at once. The reader pulls detail where they want it instead of scrolling past what they don't. Each expansion can itself be stretched, so an article becomes a tree you explore rather than a line you follow.

This demo uses canned expansions; the extension generates them live.

Install (unpacked)

  1. Clone or download the repository.
  2. Open chrome://extensions, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, select the folder.
  3. Pin the extension, open its popup, and sign in with Pollinations (device flow).
  4. Enable it on a site, then click any sentence.
